The incident summary states that on **22/02/2020 at about 08:00** in front of **Shashikant Upadhyay’s house, Govindganj, East Champaran**, the named accused acted together against **Jagmati Kunwar, Kiran Devi, and Respondent No. 2**. The evidence inventory confirms the presence of **eyewitnesses/bystanders** (“others intervened”) and identifies the complainant and victims on record.

**Section 2: Defense Submissions & Reasonable Doubts**  
The defense correctly points out that there is **no medical report, forensic report, CCTV, seizure memo, or recovery memo** on the record. That weakens proof on the extent of injuries and on physical recovery of the gold chain. The absence of such corroboration also means the Court must be cautious in accepting any aggravated inference not supported by the record. Further, the record does not individually itemize each accused’s precise act; the case is framed as a collective assault. These gaps reduce certainty on individual attribution, but they do not erase the core occurrence because the complainant and victims are identified, the episode is described with detail, and the accused themselves are named as participants in a joint incident.

**Section 3: Judicial Finding & Verdict Rationale**  
On the totality of the record, the charges are proved at least to the standard applicable on this simplified verdict record. The collective conduct alleged and not effectively rebutted establishes **common participation (IPC 149)**. The assault and humiliation of the women victims support **IPC 323, IPC 341, IPC 354, and IPC 354B**, while the snatching of the complainant’s gold chain supports **IPC 379**. The abusive and threatening conduct supports **IPC 504 and IPC 506**. The allegation of witch-branding is directly covered by the **Prevention of Witch (Daain) Practices Act, 1999 Sections 3/4**. Although the absence of medical and recovery documents prevents the Court from giving full weight to every detail with the highest degree of corroboration, the narration is internally consistent, victim-specific, and sufficient on this record to return a finding of **guilty**.
