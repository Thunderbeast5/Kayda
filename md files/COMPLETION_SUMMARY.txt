THE 6 LEGAL LAYERS:

Layer 1: Substantive Law
  └─ "What crime is committed?"
  └─ Example: NDPS Section 20 (Possession)

Layer 2: Procedural Law
  └─ "How is the case processed?"
  └─ Example: BNSS Sections 176-182 (Investigation)

Layer 3: Evidence Law
  └─ "What evidence is admissible?"
  └─ Example: BSA Sections 25-26

Layer 4: Constitutional Layer
  └─ "What rights apply?"
  └─ Example: Article 21 (Right to Life)

Layer 5: Special Restrictions
  └─ "Are there statute-specific limitations?"
  └─ Example: NDPS Section 37 (Bail restrictions)

Layer 6: Judicial Guidelines
  └─ "What do courts usually decide?"
  └─ Example: Sharad v. State (Bail principles)

                           HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

BEFORE (Old Statute Router):

  Case Input
    ↓
  Classify → NDPS
    ↓
  Output: Just "NDPS"
    ↓
  Problem: Downstream stages don't know about:
    • Procedural rules (BNSS)
    • Evidence rules (BSA)
    • Constitutional constraints (Article 21)
    • Special restrictions (Section 37)


AFTER (New Legal Regime Router):

  Case Input
    ↓
  Build Legal Graph:
    • Layer 1: Substantive = NDPS_20
    • Layer 2: Procedural = BNSS_176-182
    • Layer 3: Evidence = BSA_25-26
    • Layer 4: Constitutional = Article_21
    • Layer 5: Special = Section_37
    • Layer 6: Guidelines = Precedents
    ↓
  Output: Complete Legal Graph
    ↓
  Benefit: Downstream stages have EVERYTHING they need
    • Know exact procedural rules
    • Know exact evidence standards
    • Know constitutional constraints
    • Know special bail restrictions
    • Know relevant precedents
